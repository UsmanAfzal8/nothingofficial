Design and interchangeable hardware

CMF Phone 1’s distinctive advantage is its adaptable design. The detachable back cover and visible screws make the phone feel different from a conventional sealed slab. For some buyers, interchangeable cases provide a way to change the appearance or replace a damaged back without replacing the entire phone. For others, the screws and modular styling matter less than a thin case and easy repair.

Phone 2 Pro continues the CMF design idea but is a newer generation with its own physical details. Check the exact case compatibility rather than assuming Phone 1 accessories fit Phone 2 Pro. Camera placement, dimensions and screw positions can change. A case made for the previous model may block a lens, button or port.

In Pakistan’s heat and dust, a removable or replaceable-looking back is not permission to expose the phone to moisture. Keep dust away from openings, do not remove parts casually, and follow the manufacturer’s guidance. If the phone is wet, dry it before charging. A water-resistance rating is not a swimming rating.

Gaming and heat in Pakistan

For gaming, compare the games you actually play. A newer chipset may help with higher settings or steadier frame rates, but network ping, thermal throttling and game optimisation still matter. Test a long session rather than judging only the first ten minutes. A hot room, mobile data and high brightness can change results.

How to compare the cameras properly

Take the same set of photos on both phones if you can: a face in shade, a bright sky, a printed document, a moving subject and a night street. View them at normal size first, then zoom in. Check focus speed, skin tone, highlight control, noise, colour consistency between lenses and how quickly the camera saves a photo.

Record a short walking video and an indoor clip with speech. Listen for wind and room echo, watch for stabilisation jumps, and check whether the exposure changes smoothly. A phone that captures a sharp still but struggles with video may not suit a content creator. If you mostly share compressed social photos, the difference may be smaller than the price gap.

Camera software can change through updates. Repeat important tests after firmware updates and keep enough storage for the original files. Do not evaluate from a seller’s edited sample, because colour grading and compression can hide the device’s actual behaviour.
