What makes Buds Pro 2 different?

The most distinctive feature is the case-mounted Smart Dial. Instead of reaching for the phone for every volume change or ANC switch, the user can rotate or press the dial after assigning actions in Nothing X. This is useful on a desk, during a commute or when the phone is inside a pocket.

The second major distinction is the dual-driver design. A larger 11mm driver handles much of the low-frequency work while a 6mm micro-planar tweeter is intended to reproduce higher detail. Driver count does not guarantee a preferred tuning; seal, source, codec and equaliser still shape the result.

ANC, LDAC, dual connection and low-lag mode add flexibility, but each has tradeoffs in battery use, wireless stability or device support. Buy the feature set you will actually use.

Dual drivers: 11mm plus 6mm

CMF Buds Pro 2 use an 11mm bass driver and a 6mm micro-planar tweeter. The larger unit can provide physical low-frequency energy, while the smaller high-frequency driver handles treble detail. A crossover and software tuning determine how the drivers share the signal.

Listeners should not judge the buds from a specification alone. Play familiar tracks at a moderate volume, test vocals, bass lines, cymbals and quiet passages, and try more than one ear-tip size. An imperfect seal can make bass disappear or make ANC seem weak.

Nothing’s official page also references Dirac Opteo tuning and Ultra Bass Technology 2.0 with adjustable bass levels. Extra bass is a preference setting, not an objective improvement. High boost can mask vocals and increase fatigue.

Hybrid ANC up to 50dB explained

Nothing advertises hybrid active noise cancellation up to a theoretical 50dB and an expanded frequency range under laboratory conditions. ANC uses microphones to estimate external sound and produces an opposing signal. It is most effective against steady low-frequency noise such as bus, fan or engine rumble.

It is less complete against sudden voices, clattering dishes, horns and high-frequency irregular sounds. Passive ear-tip sealing contributes substantially. The headline value is not a promise that every listener hears 50dB less sound in every environment.

Use transparency mode when crossing roads, speaking to someone or listening near traffic. ANC can reduce awareness of surroundings. The right mode is a safety and comfort decision, not only a sound-quality setting.

ANC in Pakistan environments

Different Pakistan locations create different tests. A steady air-conditioner or bus engine is a useful ANC scenario. A busy bazaar, rickshaw, horn, generator and nearby conversation combine predictable and unpredictable noise.

Test at a safe volume and do not use earbuds to overpower dangerous street noise. For public transport, transparency can help at stops while ANC may be useful during the ride. Wind noise can also change the microphone signal and reduce comfort.

ANC performance depends on fit. If the earbuds do not seal, increasing the ANC level cannot compensate fully. Try the included tips and clean the contact surfaces.

LDAC, AAC and SBC

Official support lists LDAC, AAC and SBC. These are Bluetooth audio codecs, and the source phone plus operating system must support the selected codec. LDAC is most relevant to compatible Android devices; iPhone users should not expect LDAC through standard iOS Bluetooth audio.

LDAC can carry more data, but it may use more battery and become less stable in congested wireless conditions depending on the selected quality mode. A reliable AAC or SBC connection can be preferable when commuting or walking near many radio devices.

Codec choice cannot restore detail removed by a low-quality source, aggressive streaming setting or poor recording. Set the streaming service to a quality appropriate for your data plan and listen at a safe level.

Bluetooth 5.3 and connection distance

Bluetooth 5.3 is the published Bluetooth version, but version number alone does not guarantee a specific range or uninterrupted audio. Phone antenna design, body position, walls, Wi-Fi congestion and the connected source matter.

Keep the phone on the same side of the body during a difficult connection, avoid covering the case antenna area and update firmware through Nothing X. If one phone is stable and another is not, compare source settings before blaming the earbuds.

Do not confuse Bluetooth range with internet range. The buds can play locally stored audio without mobile data, while streaming depends on the phone’s network.

Smart Dial: the main everyday advantage

The Smart Dial is configured in Nothing X rather than being assumed to have a fixed default function. Rotation can control volume; presses can switch ANC, play or pause, skip tracks, call an assistant, answer calls, mute the microphone or toggle Low Lag Mode.

Customisation is useful because different users value different actions. A commuter may assign rotation to volume and a press to transparency. A remote worker may prioritise microphone mute. A gamer may want quick low-lag mode.

Learn the gesture sequence before using it in a call. Accidental double or triple actions can be confusing if the case is handled while the earbuds are inside.

Battery life: read the test conditions

Nothing’s support information lists up to 43 hours with the case and up to 11 hours for the earbuds using AAC with ANC off under specified laboratory conditions. A separate LDAC result is lower, and ANC, transparency, calls, volume and temperature affect runtime.

“Up to” is not a daily guarantee. Battery drain rises when the microphones, radio, ANC processor or higher-volume drivers work harder. A poor seal can also tempt a user to raise volume, increasing consumption.

Measure your own normal use across several sessions. Note codec, ANC mode, volume, calls and whether the case was charged from empty. One short commute cannot establish long-term battery health.

Call quality and six microphones

CMF Buds Pro 2 use six high-definition microphones and Clear Voice Technology 2.0, with wind-noise reduction features described by Nothing. More microphones give the processing system more signals, but they cannot make every call clear in traffic.

Test a voice note outdoors, indoors and beside a fan. Ask the other person whether your voice is natural, distant, clipped or overwhelmed by wind. Different calling apps may apply their own noise suppression.

Microphone performance also depends on how the stems sit and whether hair, clothing or wind covers the openings. Keep the phone connection stable and update firmware before diagnosing.

Low-lag gaming mode

Nothing states latency can be reduced to as low as 120ms when the buds are paired with Nothing phones and Game Mode is enabled. Wireless audio still has end-to-end delay from touch input, game rendering, Bluetooth transmission and audio playback.

Low-lag mode is useful for casual gaming and video timing, but it is not the same as wired audio and does not improve phone FPS or network ping. It may change battery use or audio processing.

Use the mode for supported games, then switch back for ordinary listening if it offers no benefit. A stable connection and comfortable fit matter more than a theoretical minimum.

Dual connection for phone and laptop

Dual connection allows Buds Pro 2 to remain linked with two devices, such as a Nothing Phone and a laptop. The support rules determine which device’s audio or call receives priority. It is not the same as mixing two streams at once.

Pair both devices, enable dual connection and test a real workflow: music on one device, a notification on the other, then an incoming call. Understand whether audio pauses, mutes or switches, and whether it returns afterwards.

When troubleshooting, disable the second connection temporarily. This distinguishes a dual-connection priority issue from a Bluetooth or app problem.

IP55: earbuds protected, case not waterproof

The earbuds have an IP55 rating under controlled IEC 60529 testing. The charging case is not designed to be waterproof. The buds are not intended for swimming, showering or submersion.

Sweat, rain and splashes still require care. Wipe the earbuds with a soft dry lint-free cloth before returning them to the case. Do not charge wet earbuds or use a hair dryer, heater or compressed air to force-dry them.

Water resistance can decline with wear, impact, openings or damaged seals. Liquid damage may not be covered by warranty, so treat IP55 as limited protection, not permission for water sports.

Comfort and ear-tip fit

Fit determines bass, passive isolation, ANC performance and stability. A tip that is too small leaks sound; one that is too large can create pressure and fatigue. Try the supplied sizes while walking and speaking.

The case and earbuds may feel different from other CMF models. Do not assume a familiar tip size transfers perfectly. Clean tips regularly and replace damaged silicone.

Stop using the buds if there is pain, rash, persistent pressure or hearing discomfort. Lower volume and take breaks. Audio equipment should not require unsafe loudness to sound full.

Sound tuning and Ultra Bass Technology

Ultra Bass Technology 2.0 provides adjustable bass levels through software. It can add impact for electronic music or films, but high levels may mask vocals and create fatigue. Start at the default setting and compare at matched volume.

Equaliser changes can make a product sound very different. Save a neutral preset for spoken audio and a preferred preset for music. Do not judge bass by raising volume; loudness can make a less balanced sound seem more impressive.

A fair listening comparison

Compare earbuds at the same volume using familiar vocal, acoustic, bass-heavy and spoken tracks. Turn ANC and transparency on and off, then repeat the test with a consistent codec. This separates tuning preference from louder playback and makes a price decision more honest.

Spatial Audio and Dirac tuning

Nothing describes a Spatial Audio Effect based on HRTF and Dirac Opteo tuning. Spatial processing can make films or compatible music feel wider, but it can also change centre vocals and reduce the natural sound of ordinary tracks.

Compare spatial mode on and off with the same short passage and matched volume. Head tracking, if offered by the source or app, can consume additional processing and is not required for every listener.

CMF Buds Pro 2 troubleshooting

If one earbud is quiet, clean the mesh gently, reseat it in the case and confirm balance settings. If pairing fails, forget the Bluetooth record, restart the phone and follow the official reset procedure. Do not insert metal into the charging contacts.

If ANC feels weak, test another tip size, clean microphones and compare a steady low-frequency sound. If calls are poor, test a voice recording and disable dual connection temporarily.

If charging fails, inspect the case port and contact pins, try a known-good cable and charge in a comfortable temperature. Do not charge wet hardware or continue with burning smell, swelling or dangerous heat.
