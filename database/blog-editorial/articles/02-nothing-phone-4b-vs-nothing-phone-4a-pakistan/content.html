Design, size and colour choices

Both phones are large. Phone 4b measures 164.4 x 78.2 x 8.6 mm and weighs 210 g; Phone 4a measures 163.9 x 77.5 x 8.5 mm and weighs 205 g. The difference is small on paper, but Phone 4a is marginally shorter, narrower, thinner and lighter. Buyers with smaller hands should still try either phone with its intended case before deciding.

Display comparison: Phone 4a wins clearly

Screen size is almost identical, but quality is not. Phone 4b has a 6.77-inch super AMOLED panel at 1080 x 2344 and 381 pixels per inch. It supports 8-bit colour, 120 Hz adaptive refresh and official peak, outdoor and typical brightness figures of 2,000, 1,200 and 600 nits respectively.

Phone 4a has a 6.78-inch flexible AMOLED panel at 1224 x 2720 and 440 pixels per inch. It lists 10-bit colour, the same headline 120 Hz adaptive refresh, and higher official brightness figures: 4,500-nit peak, 1,600-nit outdoor and 800-nit typical. It also lists a higher touch-sampling ceiling and 2160 Hz PWM versus 480 Hz on Phone 4b.

Maximum brightness numbers are measured under defined conditions and should not be treated as all-day manual brightness. Even so, the specification gap favours Phone 4a for outdoor use, fine text, high-resolution photos and richer gradients. Phone 4b should still feel smooth in ordinary scrolling because of its 120 Hz support, but buyers who watch a lot of video, edit photos or keep a phone for years will more easily justify the 4a display.

Cover glass also differs: Phone 4b lists Dragontail Pro Glass, while Phone 4a lists Gorilla Glass 7i. Neither name makes a screen indestructible. Keep the factory-applied protector until you have a suitable replacement, and use a raised-edge case if drops are likely.

Performance and storage: Phone 4a has more headroom

Phone 4b uses Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 4, built on a 4 nm process, with a stated CPU speed up to 2.3 GHz, LPDDR4x memory and UFS 2.2 storage. Phone 4a uses Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 on a 4 nm process, reaches up to 2.7 GHz and pairs LPDDR4x memory with faster UFS 3.1 storage.

Phone 4a is therefore the safer choice for demanding games, large photo libraries, frequent app installation, video editing and longer-term multitasking. Faster storage affects more than benchmark charts: it can improve installation, file transfer, app launch and operations that read or write substantial data. Actual performance still depends on thermal conditions, software versions and available storage.

Phone 4b is not designed to be unusable or slow. Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 should suit communication, navigation, video, banking, shopping and mainstream social apps. Its value depends on whether those are genuinely your main tasks. Buying unused performance is wasteful, but underbuying for a three-year gaming or creative workload creates frustration.

Capacity availability also differs. The international 4b record lists 8+128 GB, with 8+256 GB marked India-only in the official specification imported into the catalog. Phone 4a lists 8+128, 8+256 and 12+256 GB. Check the exact regional SKU instead of assuming every advertised memory combination is globally available.

Rear cameras: Phone 4a is much more versatile

Phone 4b has a 50 MP main camera with a 1/2.76-inch sensor, f/1.8 aperture, optical and electronic stabilisation, plus an ultrawide camera. Its official headline includes 4K video. This is a practical setup for everyday wide shots, documents, people and casual video, but it does not include a dedicated optical telephoto camera.

Phone 4a uses a larger 1/1.57-inch 50 MP main sensor with OIS and adds a 50 MP periscope camera. The telephoto lists 3.5x optical zoom, 7x in-sensor zoom and up to 70x ultra zoom, plus an ultrawide. The value is not the extreme 70x number alone. A dedicated longer focal length gives portraits, events, architecture and distant details more framing flexibility without walking dangerously close or relying entirely on digital cropping.

Sensor size, lens, stabilisation, processing and shooting conditions matter more than megapixels in isolation. The 4a’s larger main sensor and additional stabilised telephoto create a meaningful hardware advantage. If camera quality is a core purchase reason, Phone 4a should be the default choice unless the local price gap is unreasonable.

Phone 4b can still be enough for buyers who mostly post daylight social images at normal magnification. Spend the saved amount only if the camera compromise is real for your use. Compare original files rather than compressed marketplace samples, and avoid treating digitally enlarged moon or sign photographs as representative of normal quality.

Selfies and video

Phone 4b lists a 16 MP front camera with an 81.8-degree field of view. Phone 4a lists 32 MP and a wider 89-degree field. Resolution alone does not guarantee better skin tones or low-light output, but the 4a specification is more favourable for group selfies, video calls and creators who frequently use the front camera.

Both official records include 4K recording at 30 frames per second, 1080p at 30 or 60 fps and 1080p slow motion at 120 fps. The rear main camera benefits from OIS on both. Phone 4a’s extra telephoto expands composition choices, while Phone 4b keeps the workflow simpler.

Creators should also account for storage. Video fills 128 GB quickly after apps, offline media and system files. If a genuine 256 GB 4a unit is available with a small price premium, capacity and faster UFS 3.1 may matter more than a bundle accessory. Neither phone advertises expandable microSD storage in the official specification.

Battery endurance versus charging speed

The international Phone 4b specification lists 5,200 mAh, while India receives a 6,000 mAh version. International Phone 4a lists 5,080 mAh, with 5,400 mAh for India. A seller quoting the Indian battery figure should also disclose that the unit is the Indian regional model. Do not combine the largest battery from one region with the connectivity or NFC assumptions of another.

On international models, Phone 4b has a modest 120 mAh capacity advantage. It also drives a lower-resolution display and uses a lower-tier processor, which may help certain workloads, but only controlled testing can establish real endurance. Signal strength, refresh behaviour, brightness, camera use, background apps and updates can easily outweigh a small capacity difference.

Phone 4a charges faster: 50 W versus 33 W for Phone 4b. Both list 7.5 W reverse wired charging. Neither official specification lists wireless charging. Buyers who recharge in short intervals during work or university may value the 4a’s speed more than the 4b’s capacity edge. Buyers who charge overnight and spend long days away from sockets may prefer the 4b strategy.

Use a compatible, reputable USB-C Power Delivery or PPS charger and cable. The wattage printed on an adapter is a ceiling, not a promise that the phone will draw that rate continuously. Temperature, battery level and protocol negotiation affect charging.

Test calls, SMS and mobile data with your SIM after delivery, and obtain a return policy for incompatible stock.

Do not pick an Indian variant solely for its larger battery without understanding its different band list and NFC note. Likewise, do not pay extra for a vague “global version” label. Ask for the actual model and box code and compare the relevant official specification.

PTA approval and warranty checks

PTA compliance applies to the genuine IMEIs of the exact phone, not to every example of a model. Dial *#06#, compare both identities with Settings and the box, and verify them through a current official PTA or DIRBS channel. A seller’s successful screenshot for another unit cannot prove yours.

Calculate PTA and ownership costs before declaring Phone 4b the cheaper option. A low non-PTA 4b listing can cost more overall than a verified Phone 4a offer once registration and warranty differences are included. Do not rely on a static tax number copied from a post; use the exact device identity and current official process.

Warranty is separate. Ask who performs service, what period applies, which components and faults are covered, and whether the invoice records the exact unit. PTA approval does not create a manufacturer or seller warranty. Conversely, a warranty card does not prove both IMEIs are compliant.

Which phone fits each type of buyer?

Choose Phone 4b if the verified total price is materially lower, your work is mostly communication and media, you prefer its unibody design, and the international 5,200 mAh battery matters more than fast charging or camera zoom. It can also suit a first Nothing phone buyer who wants the software experience without paying for 4a capabilities they will not use.

Choose Phone 4a if you keep phones for several years, game regularly, edit media, value outdoor display readability, shoot portraits or events, use the selfie camera often, need 256 GB options, or rely on short fast-charging sessions. Its advantages touch daily experience, not only one benchmark.

Existing Phone 4a owners should usually keep it. Phone 4b is a lower-positioned alternative, not a broad upgrade. Moving from 4a to 4b gives up the telephoto camera, sharper display, faster platform, faster storage, higher front-camera resolution and 50 W charging. A particular design or battery preference would need to outweigh the financial loss of switching.

Performance-focused buyers may also compare Nothing Phone 3a and the wider Nothing and CMF phone collection. Compare verified local stock rather than assuming a newer model number always means a higher tier.

Repair economics also deserve attention. Ask the warranty provider about display, battery and camera-module availability before purchase. Final comparison checklist before ordering

  1. Write down whether display, camera zoom, gaming, battery or price is your first priority.
  2. Compare the same RAM, storage, region, colour, PTA condition and warranty.
  3. Confirm Phone 4b or Phone 4a battery capacity for that regional variant.
  4. Check both IMEIs and match the phone, box and invoice.
  5. Test your own SIM for calls, LTE data and VoLTE where possible.
  6. Confirm what is included, especially case, protector, cable and charger.
  7. Read the return window for region, PTA or condition mismatches.
  8. Calculate the complete cost instead of comparing headline prices.

This checklist prevents a common comparison error: selecting Phone 4a for its superior hardware, then accepting a questionable non-PTA or unsupported unit because the listing looks cheap. It also prevents paying a large premium for Phone 4b merely because it is newer. The quality of the exact offer matters alongside the quality of the model.

Should Pakistan buyers wait?

Do not wait for the global announcement; it has happened. Wait only when the Pakistan offer is not yet decision-grade. That means the seller cannot identify the region, storage and colour; both IMEIs cannot be verified; PTA cost is unknown; warranty terms are vague; or Phone 4b’s final total remains too close to Phone 4a.

Buy when the exact unit is clear, the price gap matches the hardware trade-offs, the phone supports your operator, and the return and warranty terms are written. Launch excitement is not a reason to accept an unidentified import. Equally, there is no benefit in waiting indefinitely after a suitable, verified offer meets your budget.

A practical rule is to price the features you will notice. If the 4a premium buys a display you use for hours, a camera you depend on and storage that avoids early replacement, it can be good value. If those advantages are theoretical for you and the 4b saves a meaningful amount after PTA, choose 4b confidently.

Final verdict

Phone 4a wins the specification comparison. Its display, Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 platform, UFS 3.1 storage, triple-camera system, 32 MP front camera and 50 W charging make it the more complete device. Phone 4b wins only where its priorities align with the buyer: lower global entry positioning, a simpler experience, distinct unibody design and slightly larger international battery.

For Pakistan, neither model wins until the offer is verified. Compare equal storage and region, check both IMEIs, separate PTA from warranty, and calculate total cost. If the local gap is narrow, Phone 4a is usually the smarter long-term purchase. If the gap is wide and you do not need zoom, high-resolution display or faster storage, Phone 4b can deliver the core Nothing experience at better value.
